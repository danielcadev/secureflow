branding in a way that suggests an unofficial distribution, service, product,
or organization is produced, sponsored, or endorsed by this project or Daniel

You may make truthful, non-confusing references such as saying that software is
compatible with SecureFlow, derived from SecureFlow, or an unmodified copy of a
specific SecureFlow release. Forks and modified distributions should use a
distinct name, clearly describe their changes, preserve the notices required by
their selected software license, and avoid presenting themselves as official
SecureFlow releases.

limit rights that applicable law provides independently of this policy. To ask
about a use not covered here, contact the project owner through the repository
