# M0.3 human-only versus SecureFlow-assisted protocol

Status: **protocol defined; no real study started and no comparative result exists**.

The normative machine-readable boundary is `secureflow-prospective-protocol-v2`. This document fixes the M0.3 activation sequence and prevents the known six-case development fixture, npm pilot, Mitiquete inventory, or synthetic API corpus from being presented as human-comparison evidence.

## Task-bounded question

On a previously unseen, explicitly authorized Rust and TypeScript holdout, does a SecureFlow-assisted human cohort improve validated recall and active analysis time without an unacceptable reduction in validated precision compared with a human-only cohort under equivalent non-treatment capabilities?

The treatment is access to the frozen SecureFlow configuration. Case bytes, time limits, environment class, network policy, non-treatment tools, authorization, and reporting requirements must be equivalent. Results from native and normalized capability lanes remain separate.

## Holdout and participants

- Freeze at least 20 opaque cases with at least 10 vulnerable cases and 10 safe controls, one principal invariant per case.
- Report Rust and TypeScript separately and predeclare authorization, filesystem, parser, webhook, and supply-chain families before results.
- Keep development, validation, and holdout lineages disjoint and audit overlap against the complete historical SecureFlow and Engine inventory.
- Remove labels, weakness names, expected locations, PoCs, and repairs from all participant-visible bytes.
- Recruit at least three participants per lane who did not author the cases. This minimum validates the operational protocol; it is not automatically sufficient for an inferential superiority claim.
- Recruit two primary adjudicators independent from the corpus authors and SecureFlow developers. A third independent adjudicator resolves preserved disagreements.

Before sealing the real protocol, a statistician or independent methodology reviewer must freeze the sample-size calculation, precision non-inferiority margin, recall/time estimands, confidence interval or randomization method, missingness treatment, and multiplicity policy. M0.3 does not invent those values after observing data. If the study uses only the minimum cohort, results remain exploratory unless the frozen power analysis supports the stated estimand.

## Execution

1. A corpus custodian freezes the label-free dataset and ground-truth commitment using the v2 dataset contract.
2. The operator seals the v2 protocol, exact environment, capabilities, randomization, and both lane configurations before any participant output exists.
3. Publish or independently timestamp the exact sealed protocol bytes before execution.
4. Each participant records exactly one outcome per case: `findings`, `no-finding`, `abstention`, or `operational-error`. There are no retries; a rerun requires a new protocol version.
5. Close and hash every raw and sealed submission before the custodian opens labels.
6. Blind lane identity from adjudicators where feasible. Adjudicators require actor control, a complete flow or invariant violation, a real trust boundary, a supported configuration, and reproducible impact before validating a vulnerability.
7. Preserve both primary decisions, disagreements, and any tie-break result. Never let the system developer silently edit an adjudication.

## Metrics and publication gate

Report TP, FN, FP, TN, validated recall, validated precision, false-positive rate, active time, wall time, tokens, cost, peak RSS, abstentions, and operational errors separately per lane, language, family, participant, and preregistered cohort. A failed, timed-out, malformed, or unavailable observation is an operational error, never zero and never `no-finding`. Abstentions are neither positive nor negative.

Do not calculate a composite winner score. Apply only the frozen estimands and uncertainty method. Publish negative, mixed, and protocol-deviation results. Until the holdout, participants, adjudication, and uncertainty gates are complete, the comparison status is `not-established`; global superiority, “always better than a human,” human replacement, and production-safety claims remain prohibited.
